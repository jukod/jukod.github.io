NCPC 2026 @ Jönköping University

NCPC is the Nordic Collegiate Programming Contest, an yearly competition involving algorithms and programming aimed at university students. Teams of 3 university students compete solving ≈ 10 programming problems in 5 hours using either C, Python, Java, C#, C++, Haskell or one of 40 other programming languages. At the end, the team with most solved problems wins. It’s quite fun! :-)

You can find further info in NCPC’s website. — https://nordic.icpc.io/

We will host it at JTH on Saturday, 3 October 2026. Students are welcome to participate.

Registration

You must register to participate in the Nordic Collegiate Programming Contest (NCPC 2026). Registration steps:

  1. find a coach (usually a lecturer from your programme).
    • JTH’s Computing students: Rudy Matela will gladly be your coach, just email him: <rudy.matela@ju.se>.
  2. form a team of up to 3 team members, having 3 is recommended.
  3. send your coach the required information for all (3) team members:
    • full name;
    • university email address (e.g.: @student.ju.se);
    • a team name
  4. coaches must follow the registration instructions for NCPC 2026:
    • See nordic.icpc.io
    • select Jönköping University as the site
  5. after the coach registers the team, team members get an email to complete their registration.

Registration is already open!

Preparing/practicing for the NCPC

The best problems for practicing leading up to the competition are the problems from past editions. These can be found in the NCPC website and the page for the local event:

  • nordic.icpc.io – global NCPC website
  • jukod.github.io/ncpc2026 – local NCPC @ JTH event page
