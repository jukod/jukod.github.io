What is the NCPC?

Nordic Collegiate Programming Contest

We host it at JU every year.

The NCPC format:

  • 👥 teams of 3 students
  • ⏱️ 5 hours
  • 🚩 ≈ 10 programming problems
  • ⌨️ solutions in: Python, C, Java, C#, C++, Haskell or 40 other languages.
  • 💻 1 computer per team
  • 🎈 the team with most problems solved wins

 

  • 1 problem solved = 1 balloon!

NCPC 2026

Where?   Here at JU / JTH.

When?   Saturday 3 October.

How are the problems?

  • Programming
  • Algorithms
  • Data Structures
  • From easy…
  • … to hard.

Example:

The “km/h” problem from NCPC 2025

  • Solutions not only have to produce the correct output ✅,

  • but also be in the optimal/expected time complexity ✅✅.

  • It’s often easy to get the correct output through brute-force or simulation (no points ❌),

  • but not so easy to do it in an optimal time complexity ✅.

Why participate?

  • Its fun! You get baloons 🎈🎈🎈!
  • A great way to practice programming: data structures, algorithms, etc.
  • Coursework: gets easier.
  • Teamwork.
  • Meet people / networking.
  • Potential travel (if you qualify)
  • Nice for your CV.
  • Meet companies (event sponsors).

How to register in the NCPC?

  1. form a team of up to 3 team members
  2. email <rudy.matela@ju.se> info for the (3) team members:
    • full name;
    • university email address (e.g.: @student.ju.se);
    • a team name.
  3. wait for an email with a link to complete registration
  4. complete the registration on the received link

Preparing/practicing for the NCPC

The best problems for practicing leading up to the competition are the problems from NCPC 2025, NCPC 2024 and NCPC 2023 (last years). Here are 6 interesting problems in order of difficulty:

  • F - FizzBuzz   🐝
  • C - Coffee Cup Combo   ☕
  • K - Knot Knowledge   🪢
  • L - Locust Locus   🦗
  • H - Highest Hill   ⛰️
  • J - Jamboree   ⛺

They are available for practice at open.kattis.com. You can submit your solutions online there, so you can check if they are really correct.

The competition stages:

  1. NCPC: Nordics
  2. NWERC: NW EU
  3. EUC: Europe
  4. ICPC: World Finals

Qualifying teams move to the next stage.

There’s an intricate web of competitions worldwide…
